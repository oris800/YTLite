## 1. Origins: when, by whom, on what models, original battery and scale

### Takeaway
The Dapar was created as part of the IDF's initial screening system in the early 1950s. Hebrew Wikipedia dates its introduction to the second half of the 1950/51 work year. It was a general-mental-ability score modeled on the psychometric testing of Western armies (US and UK), specifically a Raven's Progressive Matrices version plus an Otis-type verbal test. It was originally reported on a 1–9 scale, later shown as 10–90. I found no source that credits a specific individual, such as Louis Guttman, with designing the Dapar itself. Guttman founded the IDF's early research unit, and Reuven Gal was the first commander of ממד"ה (est. 1980), decades after the Dapar existed.

### Cited Findings
- **Founding period:** "מערכת המיון לצה"ל עוצבה בראשית שנות החמישים, עם מיסודו של צה"ל." The system was designed as a fast, cheap, "easy to operate" coarse hierarchical sorting tool. It separated future privates (חוגרים), NCOs (מש"קים) and officers, and these categories were called "קבוצות איכות" (low/medium/high) → קב"א. [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, pp. 70–71](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **Original Kaba components:** The Kaba was first computed from three components: סימול השכלה (years of schooling), סימול עברית (Hebrew-language level) and the דפ"ר, "שהינו אומדן כושר שכלי (המבוסס על מבחנים פסיכומטריים המשקפים יכולת שכלית כללית)". The author notes that psychometric tests of mental ability "היו הדרך המקובלת לביצוע מיון ושימשו בצבאות מערביים, כדוגמת צבא ארה"ב וצבא בריטניה". [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 72](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **Test content (Gal):** Reuven Gal (former IDF chief psychologist) describes the Primary Psychotechnical Rating (PPR) as "an intelligence evaluation score ranging from 10 to 90. It is derived from a version of the Raven's Progressive Matrices and an Otis type of verbal test. When compared to a conventional intelligence measurement, a PPR of 90 is roughly equal to an IQ of 135. The PPR constitutes about one third of the General Quality Score." Source: Gal, "Personality and Intelligence in the Military: The Case of War Heroes," in *International Handbook of Personality and Intelligence* (Plenum, 1995), pp. 731–732, citing Gal 1986. [ACADEMIC] — [Gal 1995 PDF](https://gwern.net/doc/iq/1995-gal.pdf)
- **Kaba definition (Gal):** Gal defines the General Quality Score (KABA) as "a composite of four components: intelligence evaluation, level of education, level of command of the Hebrew language, and a motivation index". Its range was **43–56** (Gal 1986). The study's data are on 1973 Yom Kippur War medal recipients. [ACADEMIC] — [Gal 1995](https://gwern.net/doc/iq/1995-gal.pdf); book: [Gal, *A Portrait of the Israeli Soldier* (Greenwood, 1986)](https://books.google.ch/books?id=y_s6_k8ngccC&pg=PA83)
- **Introduction date and original 1–9 scale:** Hebrew Wikipedia says the tests were introduced in the second half of the 1950/51 work year. Ratings were on a 1–9 scale grouped low (1–3), medium (4–6) and high (7–9). Scores 5 and 6 were split into sub-levels A/B, which mattered for officer candidates. Candidates below 3 were not drafted. Wikipedia cites Shagai Turgan, *ממני אישית לא יצא גנרל* (Yad Ben-Zvi), p. 84. [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia: דירוג פסיכוטכני ראשוני](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99)
- **Israel Police use:** The Dapar was also used by the Israel Police. The police minimum was 3, raised to 5 in the mid-1960s (Wikipedia cites *Davar*, 15 Jan 1967). [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99)
- **Louis Guttman:** After the IDF was founded, Guttman established a research unit within it. That unit became the basis of the Israel Institute for Applied Social Research, which he headed until his death in 1987. He had previously been an expert consultant to the US Army research branch in WWII (origin of the "Guttman scale"). Wikipedia does **not** say he designed selection tests. [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia: לואי גוטמן](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9C%D7%95%D7%90%D7%99_%D7%92%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9E%D7%9F)
- A review article on Israeli military psychology describes four developmental phases, the first being "establishment of the military" (1947–1966). It lists a 1949 Hebrew publication by Guttman from the IDF behavioral-science unit. [SNIPPET] — [ResearchGate: "Military Psychology in the Israel Defense Forces: A Perspective of Continuity and Change"](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249775696_Military_Psychology_in_the_Israel_Defense_ForcesA_Perspective_of_Continuity_and_Change)
- **ממד"ה:** The Behavioral Sciences Corps (מערך מדעי ההתנהגות) was established in **1980** as the successor of יחפ"צ. Its first commander was Col. Reuven Gal. [WIKI/SNIPPET] — [he.wikipedia: מערך מדעי ההתנהגות (צה"ל)](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%9A_%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%99_%D7%94%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A0%D7%94%D7%92%D7%95%D7%AA_(%D7%A6%D7%94%22%D7%9C))
- **Historical-sociological literature cited by Halperin-Porat:** Motti Safrai (2006), "השורשים החברתיים והארגוניים של המיון בצה"ל", *כתב העת לפסיכולוגיה צבאית*; E. ### Inferences
- The 1–9 → 10–90 scale with mean ≈50 and SD ≈20 (see §4) matches a **stanine** (standard-nine) scale ×10. Stanines have mean 5 and SD ≈2 and were standard in WWII-era US military testing. This is an inference from the scale's shape. No source explicitly says the Dapar was built as a stanine.
- The Raven + Otis-type verbal combination places the Dapar in the US/UK WWII group-testing tradition. Arthur Otis's group test underlay the US Army Alpha (1917), and Raven's matrices were used by British War Office Selection Boards. The Otis–Army Alpha and Raven–WOSB links are general history of testing. None of the sources found here ties the Dapar *directly* to Army Alpha/Beta or the AGCT.
- Gal's 4-component Kaba (with Hebrew and motivation) describes the pre-1980s formula. This fits Halperin-Porat's account that Hebrew was dropped in the early-1980s revision (§2).

### Gaps
- I found no source naming the individual(s) who designed the original Dapar items, and no source confirming a Guttman role in the Dapar specifically. The likely sources are Safrai 2006, Lerer 2009 and Kedem 2009, which were not accessible online.
- I found no source describing the exact original battery (number of subtests, item counts, time limits) in the 1950s.
- I found no direct evidence for modeling on Army Alpha/Beta or the AGCT.

## 2. Evolution: major revisions, computerization, adaptive testing, 2010s–2020s reforms, post-2023 changes

### Takeaway
Major milestones were:
- early 1950s: creation
- 1960s: interview-based Tzadak added to the Kaba for men
- early 1980s: Kaba/Dapar re-built and re-validated, with Hebrew dropped from the Kaba
- 2000s: computerized testing
- end of 2009: court ruling forcing score disclosure
- 2013–2015: "תר"ש המיון" transformation planned
- 2020: new Dapar tests implemented
- 11 Aug 2021: Kaba-replacement reform announced. From 1 Oct 2021 the adaptive Dapar, calculators, retests and appeals were introduced, and tzav rishon time was cut from 7 to 4 hours
- 2022: Kaba display changed to levels
- Sept 2024: numeric Kaba briefly re-displayed, then removed again

I found no confirmed official change to the Dapar *test itself* since 2023. There are claims of a 2026 retest-rule change, which are unverified.

### Cited Findings
- **1960s – Tzadak added:** "לשלושה מרכיבים אלו נוסף בשנות ה־60, לגברים בלבד, מרכיב נוסף בשם 'ציון דירוג כללי ראשוני' (צד"כ), שנועד במקור לנבא הסתגלות והצלחה בשירות בתפקידי לחימה". It is based on a structured personal interview covering social ties, adaptation to frameworks and independent functioning. [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 72](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **Early 1980s revision:** The Kaba "עודכן בתחילת שנות ה־80 בעקבות זיהוי שחיקה זוחלת ביכולת ההבחנה שלו". The erosion came from the expansion of compulsory education, which reduced the discriminating power of years of schooling, and from rising Hebrew levels as the share of immigrants fell. "מדד הקב"א החדש לא כלל את מדד העברית". The new Kaba consisted of mental ability, "דפ"ר – שנבנה ותוקף מחדש", education, and Tzadak for men. For women it had only Dapar and education. The re-construction and validation used **a male-only sample**. The footnote cites "פרויקט המיון האכ"אי, 1984". [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 73](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **2000s – computerization:** The tests became computerized, and the IDF used four tests for candidates. Wikipedia cites State Comptroller Annual Report 53A (2002), pp. 129–130. [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99)
- **2000s – Yom HaMa'ah:** In the first decade of the 2000s an occupational-fit screening day, "יום המא"ה" (מיון, אבחון והתאמה), was introduced for women. Its simulations cover seven occupational environments: הדרכה, טיפול באדם, טכני אחזקה, טכני הפעלה, מנהל וארגון, עיבוד מידע, שדה. A ממד"ה study from 2006 found the initial screening predicted general adjustment well but occupational success less well. [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, pp. 73–74](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)

[OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 77](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf). ### Inferences
- The "new Dapar" was developed and validated in 2019–2020 under תר"ש המיון (Halperin-Porat says it was being implemented during 2020). It was **publicly launched** with the reform package on 1 Oct 2021, together with adaptivity, calculators, retests and appeals.
- The 2021 media framing of "the end of the Dapar/Kaba" overstated things. The Dapar continued as a cognitive score, renewed and adaptive. The Kaba survived as a planning and officer-track index, and its public display changed in 2022 and again around 2024.
- The re-validation in the early 1980s used only men. This is the IDF's own admission, and it supports later fairness critiques, e.g., Lerer's *הקוד האתני* (2021), cited in Wikipedia.

### Gaps
- I found no official confirmation of any change to Dapar *content or format* between 2023 and late 2026. The Israel Hayom post-war article and mitgaisim were inaccessible.
- I found no exact date for the move from paper-and-pencil to computerized testing, only "2000s" via Wikipedia citing the 2002 Comptroller report.
- I could not establish whether the planned executive-function and spatial tests were actually added at the tzav rishon after 2020.

## 3. Current structure (late 2026): subtests, items, timing, languages, format

### Takeaway
As of 2026 the Dapar is a **computerized, adaptive** test taken at the recruitment office (לשכת גיוס) during the tzav rishon. Everyone starts at medium difficulty, and difficulty rises after correct answers and falls after errors. It has four or five timed sections: verbal analogies, figural analogies/figural reasoning, quantitative reasoning, and understanding (verbal) instructions. Each section has about 10–30 items, and the total is about 40 minutes. These specifics come mainly from prep sites paraphrasing mitgaisim's "לומדת דפ"ר אדפטיבי". The 2021 Walla report independently confirms analogies, verbal instructions and quantitative sections. Languages: the IDF said in 2021 the test would be translated into several languages, and a prep site claims seven languages.

### Cited Findings
- **Sections (2021 news):** The Dapar has sections including analogies, verbal instructions (הוראות מילוליות) and quantitative reasoning. Section scores are shown on the candidate's personal site. [NEWS] — [Walla, 11 Aug 2021](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/3453639)
- **Adaptive format and sections (prep, updated 30 Aug 2026):** The page says it summarizes mitgaisim's "לומדת דפ״ר אדפטיבי". The format is computerized and adaptive: all start at medium, correct → harder, wrong → easier, and the scoring formula is not published. Sections listed: אנלוגיות מילוליות, אנלוגיות צורניות, חשיבה צורנית, חשיבה כמותית, הבנת הוראות. There are 10–30 questions per section, about 40 minutes total, an overall score of 10–90, and section scores of 1–9. [PREP] — [tzavrishonplus.co.il](https://www.tzavrishonplus.co.il/guides/adaptive-dapar-2026)
- **Four-section version (prep, 11 Jul 2026):** Four sections (verbal analogies, figural analogies, quantitative reasoning, understanding instructions), 10–30 questions each, about 40 minutes, "available in 7 languages", adaptive from medium difficulty. Mitgaisim is cited as the source. [PREP] — [arielrosenberg.co.il](https://www.arielrosenberg.co.il/blog/dapar-madrich-mlaa)
- **Other prep descriptions:** A computerized test with "שאלות בצורות, בהבנה מילולית ובחשיבה כמותית", with practice sets drawn "מארבעת פרקי הדפ״ר". The site also says each request and retest is handled via mitgaisim. [PREP] — [karinkogan.co.il](https://karinkogan.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8/)
- **Calculators** have been permitted since Oct 2021. [WIKI citing mitgaisim] — [he.wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99)
- **Languages:** In 2021 the IDF said the test would "be translated into several languages". Before that, non-Hebrew speakers took a non-verbal visual version. [NEWS] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-recruiting-revamp-idf-turns-to-data-science-ends-one-dimensional-scoring/). An organization serving immigrant and Garin Tzabar recruits describes the Dapar as "based on four tests which can be taken in a variety of languages". [NGO/PREP] — [garinmahal.com](https://garinmahal.com/kaba-dapar)
- **Tzav rishon flow:** The first part is done from home in the mitgaisim personal area (questionnaires), and candidates who have not completed it are not admitted to the recruitment office. [PREP/SNIPPET] — [adrenalin-israel.co.il](https://adrenalin-israel.co.il/%D7%A6%D7%95-%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9F/) (attribution among prep results uncertain)
- **Other durations claimed:** Prep sites variously give 40–60 or about 50 minutes, and 4 or 5 sections. Some include a fifth "חשיבה צורנית" section, while others say it now appears only in an adapted test. [PREP — conflicting] — [adrenalin-israel.co.il](https://adrenalin-israel.co.il/%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8/); [pazatsta.co.il](https://pazatsta.co.il/%D7%A6%D7%95-%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9F/%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%95%D7%A7%D7%91%D7%90-%D7%97%D7%93%D7%A9%D7%99%D7%9D/); [machon-noam](https://www.machon-noam.co.il/%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%97%D7%9F-%D7%A6%D7%95-%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%95%D7%92%D7%9E%D7%90)

### Inferences
- The core content has stayed recognizably similar to Gal's description (Raven-type figural matrices plus Otis-type verbal "following instructions"), with analogies and quantitative items added. The figural sections are the descendants of the Raven component, and הבנת/הוראות מילוליות is the descendant of the Otis-type verbal test.
- Under an adaptive design, the number of items seen differs between examinees. That explains why prep sources give ranges (10–30 per section) rather than fixed counts.

### Gaps
- I found no official (IDF/mitgaisim) text describing the late-2026 subtests, item counts, time limits or language list. mitgaisim is geo-blocked. The "7 languages" claim and exact language list (Hebrew, Arabic, Russian, Amharic, English, French, Spanish?) are unverified. Arabic versions, e.g., for Druze/Circassian candidates, are undocumented in the sources found.
- I found no official statement on whether the 4- or 5-section version is current, or whether reading comprehension was permanently removed.

## 4. Scoring and use in screening and placement (Kaba, Tzadak, interview, education, Hebrew; thresholds)

### Takeaway
The Dapar is reported on a 9-point scale shown as 10–90 in steps of 10. Wikipedia gives mean 50 and SD ≈20. Section scores are 1–9. Historically it was the largest single component of the Kaba (41/43–56). Later weights were about 50% Dapar, 33% Tzadak/interview and 17% years of schooling; for immigrants with no interview, 60% Dapar and 40% education. Earlier eras differed: about one-third per Gal, and Hebrew was included until the early 1980s. Since the 2021 reform, placement is meant to use unit-specific fit scores, with the Kaba kept as a planning and officer-track index. The commonly cited thresholds are:
- Kaba 52–56 with Dapar ≥50 for officer/elite suitability
- Dapar ≥50 for some professions
- Dapar 60–70 for officer course, 8200 and pilots (prep-site claims, inconsistent)

### Cited Findings
- **Scale:** Nine values shown as 10–90. Mean 50, standard deviation about 20 (rounded because scores are multiples of 10). The score "is not necessarily final and may change until enlistment". [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99)
- **IQ equivalence:** "A PPR of 90 is roughly equal to an IQ of 135". [ACADEMIC] — [Gal 1995](https://gwern.net/doc/iq/1995-gal.pdf)
- **Kaba weights (Wikipedia):**
  - Dapar 50%, צד"כ (personal interview) 33%, שנו"ל (years of education) 17%.
  - New immigrants have no interview, so the Kaba is 60% Dapar and 40% education.
  - Since Nov 2017 women also have an interview (צמ"א) with the same formula.
  - Kaba range 41–56: 41–43 below threshold/special enlistment; 44–47 regular service; 48–51 suitable for command; 52–56 elite roles and officer corps "בשילוב דפ"ר 50 ומעלה".
  
  [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia: מדד קב"א](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%93_%D7%A7%D7%91%22%D7%90)
- **Kaba (Walla 2021):** range 41–56, based on years of schooling, a personal interview and "50% of the Dapar score"; above 52 considered high. [NEWS] — [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/3453639)
- **Other weight claims:** About half the Kaba, "50% of the kaba for men and 60% for women" (pre-2017/2019 women's formula with no interview). [NGO/PREP] — [garinmahal.com](https://garinmahal.com/kaba-dapar). Gal's earlier account puts the PPR at "about one third" of the KABA (range 43–56) when it included Hebrew and motivation. [ACADEMIC] — [Gal 1995](https://gwern.net/doc/iq/1995-gal.pdf)
- **Name of צד"כ:** In the IDF's own text it is "ציון דירוג כללי ראשוני" (צד"כ), an interview-based adjustment index for men. It is to be replaced by the all-candidate interview "צמ"א – ציון מיון אישי" (also glossed as ציון התאמה לשירות), which has been in the women's Kaba since Nov 2019. [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, pp. 72, 79–80](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **Post-reform placement logic:** Placement is to combine a profession-fit score (a formula over thinking skills, occupational skills, adaptive traits and interpersonal patterns relevant to each profession) with the candidate's motivation score and an IDF priority ranking. The Kaba remains for officer-track planning. [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 80](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf). Combat placement has historically been determined "כמעט באופן בלעדי על בסיס מדדי הכשירות הרפואית והמוטיבציה" (except special elite units). [OFFICIAL] — [Halperin-Porat 2021, p. 71](https://www.idf.il/media/wfydns5b/%D7%94%D7%98%D7%A8%D7%A0%D7%A1%D7%A4%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%9E%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%94.pdf)
- **Unit-specific algorithms:** Each combat role gets its own algorithm, so a recruit can score high for Armor and low for the Navy. [NEWS] — [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/in-recruiting-revamp-idf-turns-to-data-science-ends-one-dimensional-scoring/); [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/3453639)
- **Officer course and Hebrew:** Low Hebrew no longer automatically disqualifies a candidate from officer course; commanders decide. [NEWS] — [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/3453639); [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/r1c3czwlk)
- **Official role requirement example:** The mitgaisim page for מאבחנת מדעי ההתנהגות requires "פרופיל 25 ומעלה, דפ"ר 50 ומעלה". [OFFICIAL/SNIPPET] — [mitgaisim.idf.il](https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/%D7%AA%D7%A4%D7%A7%D7%99%D7%93%D7%99%D7%9D/%D7%9E%D7%90%D7%91%D7%97%D7%A0%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%A2%D7%99-%D7%94%D7%94%D7%AA%D7%A0%D7%94%D7%92%D7%95%D7%AA/)
- **Prep-site threshold claims (unverified, inconsistent):**
  - "ציון הדפ"ר המינימלי לקצונה הוא 60"
  - 8200: Kaba ≥54 and Dapar ≥60 officially, "in practice" 70+
  - pilot course: Dapar ≥60 with Kaba ≥54 per one site, Dapar ≥70 with medical profile ≥97 per others
  
  [PREP] — [machon-noam (8200)](https://www.machon-noam.co.il/%D7%A6%D7%94%D7%9C-%D7%94%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%94-8200); [8200academy](https://8200academy.com/%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99-%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%9E%D7%94-%D7%94%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%A9%D7%9C%D7%9A-%D7%90%D7%95%D7%9E%D7%A8/); [irpa.co.il](https://irpa.co.il/articles/idf-pilot-course-admission-conditions.php); [tzav-r.co.il](https://tzav-r.co.il/%D7%A7%D7%95%D7%A8%D7%A1-%D7%98%D7%99%D7%A1/) (search-summary attribution of exact figures to specific sites is approximate)
- **Empirical datum:** In Gal's study of 1973 war medal recipients, the mean PPR was 71.81 for medalists vs 69.23 for matched controls (n.s.), and the mean KABA was 53.97 vs 53.20. [ACADEMIC] — [Gal 1995](https://gwern.net/doc/iq/1995-gal.pdf)
- **Critique:** Sociologist Zeev Lerer (*הקוד האתני*, Van Leer 2021) argues the Kaba/Dapar measure social and cultural background. [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia: מדד קב"א](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%93_%D7%A7%D7%91%22%D7%90)

### Inferences
- With a mean of 50 and SD ≈20 on a stanine-like scale, Dapar 70 is roughly the top ~23% and Dapar 90 roughly the top ~4% (standard stanine percentages). This is an inference from the scale's shape, not a published IDF distribution.
- Combat placement for regular units historically depended on medical profile and motivation more than on the Dapar. The Dapar mainly gates officer tracks, intelligence/technology and other "quality" roles.

### Gaps
- I found no official, current list of Dapar cutoffs for officer course, pilots, 8200 or technology units. All specific numbers above 50 come from prep sites and conflict.
- The current Kaba weights after the 2021 reform and the Nov 2019 women's change are not confirmed by an official 2024–2026 source. Wikipedia's 50/33/17 is the best available.
- I found no official statement on whether the Hebrew score (סימול עברית) still enters any formula. Per Halperin-Porat it was removed from the Kaba in the 1980s, but it remains a separately collected measure, updated 2017–2018.

## 5. Retesting (דפ"ר חוזר): official policy, conditions, frequency

### Takeaway
Until 2021 the Dapar was essentially one-shot. Since the 2021 reform, candidates may request a single retest through mitgaisim, after completing Yom HaMa'ah. Per mitgaisim's policy as quoted in search results and by prep sites (mid-2026), only those with **Dapar ≤70** are eligible. Approval is automatic for strong school grades and otherwise discretionary based on documents and recommendations. Sources conflict on whether the highest or the latest score counts. A claim that the threshold was raised to ≤80 without documents in August 2026 is **unverified**.

### Cited Findings
- **Before 2021:** Each candidate originally had one chance and could not retake. Police candidates could retake after a year. Since 2021 IDF low scorers may retest. [WIKI] — [he.wikipedia](https://he.wikipedia.org/wiki/%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A8%D7%95%D7%92_%D7%A4%D7%A1%D7%99%D7%9B%D7%95%D7%98%D7%9B%D7%A0%D7%99_%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%A0%D7%99). At the tzav rishon the IDF "כמעט ולא אפשר" retests before 2021. Retests for low scorers were announced, with school-principal input and use of gap-year or pre-army deferral periods. [NEWS] — [ynet](https://www.ynet.co.il/news/article/r1c3czwlk)
- **Official policy page:** mitgaisim has a page titled "מדיניות מבחן דפ"ר חוזר". A search summary of it states that review and approval of a retest is given only to those whose Dapar is 70 or below (inclusive), and that the **higher** score counts. [OFFICIAL/SNIPPET — page geo-blocked; not read directly] — [mitgaisim: מדיניות מבחן דפ"ר חוזר](https://www.mitgaisim.idf.il/%D7%9B%D7%AA%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA/%D7%9B%D7%AA%D7%91%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%A8-%D7%94%D7%96%D7%9E%D7%9F/%D7%A6%D7%95-%D7%A8%D7%90%D7%A9%D7%95%D7%9F/%D7%9E%D7%93%D7%99%D7%A0%D7%99%D7%95%D7%AA-%D7%9E%D7%91%D7%97%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8-%D7%97%D7%95%D7%96%D7%A8/)
- **Prep account (11 Jul 2026, citing mitgaisim):**
  - "רק מי שציון הדפ"ר שלו 70 ומטה (כולל)"
  - automatic approval for an average ≥80 in STEM subjects or ≥90 in humanities, or ≥90 in 3-unit math plus ≥90 humanities
  - others reviewed on recommendations and documents
  - submitted to the service center (מיט"ב) with an updated grade sheet
  - retest once only, and the retest cannot be appealed
  - "הציון האחרון הוא הקובע", meaning a lower retest score replaces the earlier one, **which contradicts** the snippet above
  
  [PREP] — [arielrosenberg.co.il](https://www.arielrosenberg.co.il/blog/dapar-madrich-mlaa)
- **Timing and documents:** A request can be filed only after Yom HaMa'ah, with a deadline shown in the personal area. Approval is not automatic. Grounds include technical errors, medical or mental condition on test day, or a new diagnosis (attention/learning), with signed professional documents. [PREP] — [karinkogan.co.il](https://karinkogan.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8/)
- One prep claim says retests are available up to half a year before enlistment. [PREP/SNIPPET] — [adrenalin-israel.co.il](https://adrenalin-israel.co.il/%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8/) (attribution approximate)
- Another prep site (edited 20 Sep 2026) says it **could not verify** the live policy. It warns "אין להניח שהציון הגבוה תמיד נשמר" and states the IDF's regular track allows one retest. [PREP] — [mivchan.co.il](https://mivchan.co.il/dapar-retest)
- **Unverified claim:** "לפי המדיניות שאתר מתגייסים פרסם באוגוסט 2026, מי שקיבל דפ"ר 80 ומטה יכול לבקש מבחן חוזר, בלי מסמכים". This appeared in a search summary from an unidentified prep site and could not be traced or confirmed. Prep sites updated in July–September 2026 still state ≤70. [UNVERIFIED] — [machon-noam (one of the result set)](https://www.machon-noam.co.il/%D7%A2%D7%A8%D7%A2%D7%95%D7%A8-%D7%A2%D7%9C-%D7%A6%D7%99%D7%95%D7%9F-%D7%93%D7%A4%D7%A8)
- **Appeals:** Since 2021 recruits have had a right to appeal screening results, e.g., a large gap between bagrut and army scores, supported by a principal's recommendation. [NEWS] — [Walla](https://www.walla.co.il/news/military/3453639)

### Inferences
- The retest system has stabilized as one discretionary retest for Dapar ≤70, linked to school-grade evidence. This makes the Dapar partly cross-checked against Ministry of Education data, which fits the 2021 plan for the Ministry to transfer grades to the IDF (ynet).

### Gaps
- Which score counts (highest or latest) is unresolved. The official page could not be read.
- The August 2026 "≤80, no documents" change is unconfirmed.
- I found no data on how many retests are requested or approved, or on average score changes.

## 6. Scale: annual number of examinees and score distribution

### Takeaway
I found no official annual count of Dapar examinees or official score distribution. The State Comptroller says the screening apparatus handles "hundreds of thousands" of youths a year. Wikipedia gives mean 50 / SD ≈20 without a cited distribution table.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- With Israeli birth cohorts of about 150–180k and Jewish/Druze/Circassian cohorts subject to call-up, plus Haredi call-ups since 2024, annual tzav rishon examinees are plausibly in the **~100,000+** range. This is an inference, not a published figure.

### Gaps
- I found no published annual number of Dapar examinees, no official score distribution, no breakdown by gender/sector, and no validity coefficients. Knesset Research Center (ממ"מ) documents on screening were not located in this session.
